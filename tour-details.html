Bali Tropical Paradise & Nusa Penida Island

Bali, Indonesia 7 Days / 6 Nights 4.9 (128 Verified Reviews)

Journey Overview

Immerse yourself in the hypnotic charm of Bali, the Island of the Gods. This bespoke 7-day expedition carefully balances adrenaline-charged adventure, tranquil wellness, and deep cultural immersion. You will stay in handcrafted luxury boutique villas in Ubud overlooking verdant rainforest valleys, before transferring to beachfront resorts in Seminyak.

From ascending the volcanic ridges of Mount Batur under the morning starlight to boarding private speedboats across the Badung Strait to the dramatic limestone arches of Nusa Penida, every day is orchestrated by native certified English-speaking guides.

Curated Tour Highlights

Private sunrise trek at Mount Batur with breakfast cooked over volcanic steam.
High-speed catamaran cruise to Nusa Penida & Kelingking T-Rex cliff exploration.
Sacred water purification ritual at Tirta Empul Temple with a village elder.
Manta Ray snorkeling safari in Crystal Bay and Gamat Bay marine reserves.
VIP infinity pool day-pass at Uluwatu Cliff Club & traditional Kecak Fire Dance.
6 nights in 5-star verified villas with daily floating breakfast and spa sessions.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

1

Day 1: Arrival in Denpasar & Ubud Rainforest Check-in

Touch down at Ngurah Rai International Airport where your personal chauffeur greets you with cold towels and refreshments. Scenic transfer through terraced hills to your private luxury villa in Ubud. Welcome Balinese floral blessing and evening orientation dinner.

2

Day 2: Sacred Monkey Forest & Campuhan Ridge Trail

Morning stroll through the lush canopy of Ubud Sacred Monkey Forest. Savor organic artisan lunch along Campuhan Ridge followed by a visit to traditional art workshops and coffee plantations tasting Luwak beans.

3

Day 3: Mount Batur Sunrise Hike & Toya Devasya Hot Springs

Early 3:00 AM departure to base camp. Ascend Mount Batur with headlamps and expert mountain guides. Witness dawn breaking over Lake Batur and Mount Rinjani. Soak tired muscles in natural geothermal hot springs overlooking the caldera.

4

Day 4: Nusa Penida Island Speedboat Day Expedition

Board a fast boat to Nusa Penida. Photograph the world-famous Kelingking Beach cliff viewpoint, swim in Broken Beach and Angel's Billabong, and snorkel with majestic manta rays in Crystal Bay.

5

Day 5: Seminyak Coastal Transfer & Sunset Beach Club

Check out from Ubud and head to coastal Seminyak. Check in to beachfront resort suite. Afternoon free for surfing lessons, boutique shopping, or beachside massage. Sunset cocktails reserved at Ku De Ta.

6

Day 6: Uluwatu Clifftop Temple & Kecak Fire Dance

Afternoon excursion to Uluwatu Temple perched 70 meters above crashing Indian Ocean waves. Watch the dramatic sunset Kecak fire performance followed by a candlelit seafood barbecue banquet on Jimbaran Beach.

7

Day 7: Balinese Souvenirs & Airport Departure

Leisurely floating breakfast and final dip in the plunge pool. Transfer to Denpasar airport with lasting memories and an album of professional photos provided by your Stackly escort.
